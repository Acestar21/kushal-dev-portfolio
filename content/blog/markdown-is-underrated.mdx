I've tried several note-taking applications over the years, but I always come back to Markdown.

## Plain text wins

Markdown files don't depend on a specific application.

I can edit them in VS Code, a terminal editor, or even GitHub's web interface.

## Example

```md
# Notes

- Keep ideas simple
- Commit often
- Document decisions
```

It's not the most feature-rich format, but it's one of the most durable.
